The Crimson Hound Foundry

Loyalty, Discipline, Tradition, Excellence.

Welcome to the Crimson Hound Foundry, a gathering place where Sith Lords, Hound Lords, Travelers, Pilots, Mechanics, Bounty Hunters, and local citizens can gather for food, entertainment, business, or Galactic conquest.

Our Foundry Founder

Darth Trak founded the foundry by tracking the necessities of travelers and workers moving through the industrial districts. Over time, it became one of the city's best known hunting and gathering places...and continues to expand.

Delivery Services

Our mechanical couriers carry meals thought the city by ground vehicle and air transport for customers who cannot visit us in person.

Entretainment Hall

Music, lightsaber exhibition matches, holo-table games, and Special Events give visitor something to experience beyond the tastiness of our food and drinks.
