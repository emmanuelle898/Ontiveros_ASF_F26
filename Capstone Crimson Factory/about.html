Our Story

About the Foundry

The Crimson Hound Foundry serves travelers and workers moving through the industrial district.

Darth Trak, Our Founder

Darth Trak founded the restaurant after tracking the needs of pilots, mechanics, and travelers moving through the district. What began as one hunting and gathering place has grown into three locations.

Delivery Service

Our mechanical couriers carry meals across the city by ground vehicle and air transport for customers who cannot visit us in person.

Entertainment Hall

Music, lightsaber exhibition matches, and special events give visitors an experience beyond our food and drinks.

Holotable Games

Crews and citizens can test their luck at our holotable while waiting for a meal or meeting friends.
